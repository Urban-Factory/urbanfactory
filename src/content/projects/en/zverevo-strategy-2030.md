## Challenge

Zverevo is a small coal-mining town in the Rostov region. Like many single-industry towns, it was looking for a new economic direction. In 2018 the town received the status of a priority development territory, which opened new tools for investment. It did not yet have a strategy that residents shared.

The city administration first asked for separate pieces of work: a park project and a town brand. Urban Factory proposed one integrated approach instead: a development strategy written together with the people who live there.

## What we did

The work started in the summer of 2018 with a participatory design and foresight workshop. In September the administration and Urban Factory signed an agreement of intent, and the strategy was split into five stages.

- **Team of Change.** We set up an interdisciplinary working group that met every week. Thematic groups worked on the image of the town, the economy and entrepreneurship, and a healthy city; groups on tourism and education followed.
- **Workshops and expertise.** Residents worked directly on large plans of the town. An international round table brought in experience from Recife, Brazil, with Prof. Circe Monteiro of UFPE.
- **Investment and partners.** We prepared material on the priority development territory for potential investors and supported the town's contacts with partner cities abroad.
- **Media.** Two videos about the town were filmed and published as part of the strategy's communication plan.

The strategy sets a mission — a park town with an innovative economy. It compares three scenarios (inertial, innovative and target) and sets four directions: a healthy city, tourism, an innovative economy and education.

## Result

All five stages were delivered and accepted. The city council adopted the Development Strategy 2030 in December 2018.

The strategy also became the starting point for the next step: the participatory design of Kolesnikov Boulevard, confirmed as the priority public space at public hearings and by the Team of Change.
