## Challenge

How do you build a comfortable home on a wooded plot without cutting down the trees? The aim was a small private house for a site in Istria, Croatia — versatile, quick to build, affordable and gentle on nature.

## What we did

The concept was developed by an unnamed studio, an architecture practice from Belgrade, in collaboration with Urban Factory.

The house is only 3 metres wide. This narrow footprint lets it slip between existing trees instead of replacing them. Inside, the plan is one long line of rooms that alternate in function: bathroom, kitchen, living room, a work space with a library, and a bedroom. Square shelves in the library double as storage, and give access to an enclosed rooftop patio that stays private from the outside.

The design is modular. The full version has 85 m², and smaller versions can be put together from the same modules, so the house can be adapted to the plot and the budget.

The presentation shows the idea from every side: exterior and interior visualisations, a floor plan, a long section, axonometric views and model views.

## Result

An architectural concept for an 85 m² timber house, presented in visualisations, plans and sections. The project is in progress.
