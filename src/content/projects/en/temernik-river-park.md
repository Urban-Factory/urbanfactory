## Challenge

The Temernik is a small river that crosses Rostov-on-Don. For decades it worked, in practice, as a drain.

To change this, a non-profit was created: the Temernik Park organisation. It is a coalition of business, experts and the public. Its aim is to rehabilitate the river and turn its banks into a city-wide ecological park, working alongside the regional programme for the ecological recovery of the Temernik basin.

## What we did

Mihail Veklenko was a co-founder of the non-profit and a member of its core team.

- **Strategy and economics.** He was responsible for how real estate and the economy around the river would work, and what the riverside spaces would hold: parks, new buildings, cycling and walking routes, places to rest. He also worked on how to involve business and the city, so that the river is cleaned and opened to people.
- **International partnership.** Mihail brought in INCITI, a research institute of the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE), and Prof. Circe Monteiro, with their experience of the 15 km Capibaribe linear park. In October 2018 the Temernik Park non-profit and INCITI signed a memorandum of cooperation.
- **Events and partners.** Mihail organised the first forum “Small Rivers in Big Cities” on 5–6 October 2018 at Don State Technical University. It included sessions on river rehabilitation and on bringing riverside areas back into the city, and a festival, “Rostov-on-Temernik”. He then prepared the concept of a second conference with participants from the BRICS countries for 2019.

## Result

Mihail stood at the start of the non-profit and of its First Mile direction. In 2019 he organised the work to launch the first section of a linear park along the Temernik. It runs for about 1–1.5 km, from the bridge at Vavilova and Sheboldaeva streets to the city zoo. The non-profit became the main public driver of this section.

The First Mile opened in spring 2023.

## Numbers

- About 1–1.5 km — the First Mile of the linear park
- 15 km — the Capibaribe park in Recife, the reference for the partnership
