## Challenge

Rostov-on-Don has a small river, the Temernik, that has long been polluted and cut off from city life. Recife in Brazil faced a similar problem with the Capibaribe — and answered it with a 15-kilometre linear park, developed by the city together with a research team from the Federal University of Pernambuco (UFPE). We wanted Rostov's river initiative to learn directly from that experience.

## What we did

**Moscow Urban Forum, 2017.** For the session "Aqua est vita: the role of the river in the modern megalopolis", Mihail Veklenko organised the participation of two speakers: Prof. Circe Monteiro, head of the INCITI research institute at UFPE, who presented the Capibaribe Park project, and Swami Jyothirmayah of the Art of Living Foundation.

**Prof. Monteiro's visit, 2018.** Urban Factory invited Prof. Monteiro to Russia and organised the whole programme: a public lecture on the Capibaribe linear park in Moscow; a talk at the forum in Rostov-on-Don; a walk and tree planting by the Temernik River; a working meeting with the Temernik Park non-profit; and meetings with Rostov universities on student exchange, joint research and training.

**Small Rivers in Large Cities, 2018.** At this first Rostov forum on the Temernik linear park, Prof. Monteiro shared how Recife built its park step by step — small public spaces, each finished on its own, joined into one green corridor.

## Result

- Recife's experience reached the people working on the Temernik River — city experts, universities and residents.
- A memorandum of cooperation between the Temernik Park non-profit and INCITI was signed in October 2018.
- Rostov universities and INCITI discussed student exchange and joint research.
- A lasting exchange between Recife and Rostov-on-Don, which fed into the Temernik River Park project.
