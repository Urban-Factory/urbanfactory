## About the film

“Opposite the Left Bank” is a documentary portrait of Rostov-on-Don. Eight young authors film the city and, as they work, change how they see it. Director Evgeny Grigoriev films the process itself, so the result is a “nesting-doll” film: a film about the city and about people discovering it.

## What was done

Urban Factory was the local partner and the organiser on the ground in Rostov-on-Don.

## Authors

Olga Bondareva, Georgy Obukhov, Maria Korenkova, Maria Ryazantseva, Alyona Savelyeva, Nadezhda Khatskevich, Alyona Popushenko, Dmitry Tsupko.
